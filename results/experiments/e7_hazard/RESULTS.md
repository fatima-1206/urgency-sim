## Reading

### At matched airtime the baseline still wins, at every hazard level

Budget 11 is the only budget where the two methods spend comparable airtime. Every cell
is negative:

| hazard | pred/sparse | pred/dense | unpred/sparse | unpred/dense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | −0.058 | −0.037 | −0.070 | −0.060 |
| 0.35 | −0.043 | −0.056 | −0.056 | −0.070 |
| 0.50 | −0.098 | −0.027 | −0.091 | −0.058 |

Phase 3's verdict is not an artefact of the hazard threshold it happened to pick.

### The hazard level controls how badly the baseline saturates — and that is informative

Lowering the hazard line puts more cells over it, so the baseline can fire more and the
budget-45 comparison gets closer to fair:

| hazard | events/run | baseline alarms @ budget 45 | alarm ratio | on-time margin @ 45, pred/sparse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | 571 | 23.4 | **2.6×** | **+0.127** |
| 0.35 | 354 | 9.8 | 5.3× | +0.178 |
| 0.50 | 170 | 5.3 | 11.0× | +0.128 |

At hazard 0.25 the baseline reaches 23.4 of the requested 45 — still short, but the
fairest matched-budget-45 comparison anywhere in this programme. The margin there is
**+0.127**, against **+0.178** at hazard 0.35 where the baseline is five times
out-spent.

**The fairer the comparison, the smaller the margin.** That is direct evidence that the
budget-45 advantage reported throughout this programme is substantially a saturation
artefact rather than a prioritisation advantage. It is not proof — hazard 0.50 breaks
the monotone pattern (+0.128 at 11× out-spend), and only three points are available —
but it points the same way as the matched-budget column, which is unambiguous.

### One genuine lead

The latency penalty at matched airtime nearly vanishes at the lowest hazard level:

| hazard | latency penalty at budget 11 (ticks, 4 cells) |
|---|---|
| 0.25 | +1.8, −5.7, +8.7, +0.1 |
| 0.35 | +12.0, +16.6, +10.9, +14.8 |
| 0.50 | +32.8, +16.6, +44.3, +40.4 |

At hazard 0.50 the method is 17–44 ticks slower than the baseline at equal airtime. At
hazard 0.25 that penalty is gone, two of four cells sitting within a tick of zero.

So the method's disadvantage shrinks as events become plentiful — the **opposite** of
the information-scarcity premise in `PROTOCOL_EXPERIMENTS.md` §1, and consistent with
E1's finding that explorability was not the driver. Whether the method would overtake
the baseline below hazard 0.25 is **untested**; this programme does not go there, and
the claim is recorded as a lead, not a result.
