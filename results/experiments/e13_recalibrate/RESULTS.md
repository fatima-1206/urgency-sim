## Verdict: the equal-airtime result is not an artefact of 8-seed calibration

**Prediction (written before the run):** the matched-cell margin stays negative and within
0.02 of the 8-seed value. **Outcome: HELD.**

### Calibration did move, as expected

Raising calibration from 8 seeds to 20 changed the triggers, and at the load-bearing
budget it changed them in the direction that matters. At hazard 0.35 the baseline's
achieved rate at budget 11 goes from **9.78** (flagged $-11\%$ off target) to **11.10**
(on target). The 8-seed calibration was genuinely under-matching the baseline there, so
those cells were slightly *less* matched than reported.

| level | method @ 11 | 8-seed achieved | 20-seed achieved |
|---|---|---|---|
| h0.25 | threshold | 11.00 | 10.90 |
| h0.35 | threshold | **9.78** (off target) | **11.10** |
| h0.5 | threshold | 5.34 | 4.94 (saturated either way) |

Mean over these 12 cells: **−0.0605 at 8 seeds, −0.0585 at 20 seeds.** The largest single
shift is +0.022 and no cell changes sign. Every one of the 24 comparisons in this
experiment (12 at budget 11, 12 at budget 5) favours the baseline.

### Budget 5 extends the result downward

Budget 5 was not run in the original programme at these levels. All 12 cells are negative,
10 of them significantly, and the margin is *larger* at the smaller budget
(−0.034 to −0.151). The deficit does not shrink as the budget shrinks.

### What this changes in the write-up

The programme's recorded limitation — *"calibration used 8 seeds where Phase 1 settled on
20"* — can now be stated as **tested rather than outstanding**. It did bias one
calibration point by about 12% of a budget, and correcting it moved the headline mean by
**0.002**. The limitation is retained in the paper as a description of what was done, but
it is no longer a threat to the conclusion.
